## Canonical tagline

> **Only variety can absorb variety.**  
> — W. Ross Ashby

**Source concept:** Law of Requisite Variety (cybernetics) — a regulator must have at least as much variety as the system it controls.

**Product reading:** Prism doesn’t “remove complexity.” It **harnesses** it by making variety *explicit and inspectable* (specialists, models, routes, steers, artifacts) so a human can steer multi-agent work.
